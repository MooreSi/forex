## Why nothing stopped it — four failures, in order of cost

### 1. The Telegram path has NO trend filter at all (-$718.95)

`GOLD DIGGERS INSTITUTIONAL` lost **-$718.95 over 20 trades, 5W/15L**, almost
all BUYs. `governor.check_pre_trade_filters` applies exactly two things:

1. minimum TP1 R:R
2. a directional cap on *concurrent unprotected* same-direction trades

**Neither is a trend filter.** Nothing on that path asks "is the higher
timeframe going the other way?" — so a channel posting BUY signals into a
falling market gets every one of them executed. The directional cap limits how
many run *at once*, not how many are taken across a day.

### 2. The Reversal Engine's bias filter was bypassed by a score that does not predict (-$219.43)

The engine DOES have the guard, in `reversal_engine_live_execute.py`:

```python
if ((fresh_htf == "bullish" and direction == "SELL" and level_score < 0.75)
        or (fresh_htf == "bearish" and direction == "BUY" and level_score < 0.75)):
```

It blocks a counter-bias trade **only when `level_score` is below 0.75.**
Today's executed signals:

| bias | direction | n | avg score | scored >= 0.75 | net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bullish | SELL | 5 | 0.89 | **5 of 5** | **-$169.63** |
| bearish | BUY | 1 | 0.87 | **1 of 1** | **-$49.80** |
| bullish | BUY | 4 | 0.73 | 2 | +$40.45 |
| bearish | SELL | 4 | 0.74 | 2 | +$56.08 |

**With the bias: +$96.53 over 8 trades. Against it: -$219.43 over 6 — and all
six cleared the bypass.**

This is the same `level_score` that `003` measured as not ranking outcomes at
all: its 0.9 band is the biggest losing band on record (400 trades, -$1,378).
**The protection is switched off by a number that does not mean what its name
suggests, precisely when the trade is counter-trend — which is when it is
needed.**

### 3. The safety net was off, or set to a number the owner did not choose

Read from `vantage_risk_settings` today:

| setting | value | should be |
|---|---|---|
| `risk_governor_enabled` | **0 (OFF)** | on |
| `max_daily_loss_pct` | **40.0** | **3.0** (his confirmed answer) |
| `circuit_breaker_enabled` | 1 | 1 |
| `circuit_breaker_losses` | 3 | 3 |

`docs/simon-handover/011` raised exactly this on 2026-08-29 and it is still
open. **A 3% daily loss limit would have halted trading long before -$1,270.**

### 4. And the daily-loss limit could not have worked anyway

`vantage_simulation_account.balance` is **-$4,875.46**. A limit expressed as a
percentage of balance has a negative threshold on a negative balance, which is
meaningless. The recorded halt reasons show the arithmetic collapsing —
`$-134322.64 today vs -$54032.46 (40.0% of $135,081.16)` and `$-178.45 today vs
-$176.59 (20.0% of $882.95)` on an account that has never held either sum.

**So the one protection sized to the size of the loss was disabled by the
corrupt balance in `reversal-engine/010`.** The circuit breaker did fire (active
until 17:49:50), but it counts consecutive losses with a cooldown, and today
still contained a run of **eight losses in a row**.

## What should have been done differently

**In order of how much they would have saved today:**

1. **A trend gate on the Telegram execution path.** The single biggest loss,
   -$718.95, came from a channel's BUY signals executing unopposed into a
   sustained downtrend. This is the largest gap and it is not tracked anywhere
   yet — raised as `reversal-engine/080`.
2. **Stop `level_score` bypassing the bias filter.** All six counter-bias
   trades cleared it. On a score that does not rank outcomes, the bypass has no
   justification: make the higher-timeframe bias a hard gate, or gate it on
   something measured to work.
3. **Set the daily loss limit to the 3% he confirmed, and fix the balance it
   is computed from.** Either alone is insufficient: 3% of a corrupt negative
   balance is still nonsense. `handover/011` + `reversal-engine/010`.
4. **Turn the risk governor on.** `handover/011`, open since 2026-08-29.
5. **Reconsider the circuit breaker for trend days.** Three consecutive losses
   with a cooldown did not prevent an eight-loss run. A day-level rule ("stop
   after N losses today", not "N in a row") is the shape that matches what
   happened.

**None of items 1, 2, 3 or 5 is an ML problem, and none is the payoff problem
in `003`.** Today was a market-fit failure: a mean-reversion system trading a
trend day with its trend protection either absent, bypassed, or switched off.

## What today does NOT show

It does not show the engine getting worse. The win rate on trades that went
*with* the higher-timeframe bias was fine. It shows what happens when a
counter-trend system meets a trending day with no working brake — and the
brakes were all individually known about before today.
